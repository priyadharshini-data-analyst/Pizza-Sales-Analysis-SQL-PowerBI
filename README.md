🍕 **Pizza Sales Analysis | SQL Server & Power BI**

Excited to share my latest Data Analytics portfolio project, where I analyzed 48,620 pizza sales records using SQL Server and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to uncover meaningful business insights.

📊 **Key Highlights:** Total Revenue: 817.86K | Total Orders: 21,350 | Total Pizzas Sold: 49,574 | Average Order Value: 38.31 | Average Pizzas Per Order: 2.32.

🔍 **What I Analyzed:** Daily and monthly sales trends, revenue contribution by pizza category and size, and the top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas based on revenue, quantity, and total orders.

🛠️ **Technical Skills Applied:** SQL Server, SQL Aggregations, GROUP BY, Date Functions, Power BI, KPI Development, Data Visualization, and Business Analysis.

💡 **Key Insights:** The Classic category generated the highest revenue, large-sized pizzas contributed the largest share of sales, and The Thai Chicken Pizza was the top revenue-generating product.

Through this project, I strengthened my ability to transform raw business data into meaningful insights and communicate findings through interactive dashboards.
